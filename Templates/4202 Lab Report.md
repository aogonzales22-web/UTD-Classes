# Abstract
In this experiment, we measured several different devices under test (DUT's) using a Vector Network Analyzer (VNA) to introduce 2-port testing beyond traditional single-port $S_{11}$ measurements. After performing a complete SOLT calibration on both ports, we captured $S_{11}$, $S_{22}$, and $S_{21}$ parameters for attenuators, coaxial filters, coaxial lines, and a microstrip line, saving all data as S2P files for Microwave Office (MWO). By evaluating our VNA measurements and MWO plots against theoretical expectations, we successfully verified the operational characteristics of each component, including signal attenuation, filter bandwidths, and microstrip reflection humps.
